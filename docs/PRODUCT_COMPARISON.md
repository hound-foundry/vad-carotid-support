# Related products and the V1 choice

Checked 12 October 2026. This is a small public-web scan, not a definitive store audit. Search results did not identify a dedicated, broad VAD/CeAD patient app; that does not prove one does not exist.

| Product | What it offers | What this project can learn |
|---|---|---|
| [BJJ Dissection](https://bjjdissection.org/) | Artery-dissection information for grapplers, with recovery questions and a planned case registry. | Make the condition-specific learning path easy to find and show recovery concerns clearly. |
| [US Department of Veterans Affairs: Stroke Recovery](https://mobile.va.gov/app/stroke-recovery) | Stroke recovery tools and resources, including appointment and recovery support. | Design around the practical needs of stroke survivors, communication and care conversations. |

V1 combines useful patterns: clear learning paths, recovery support, carer material and appointment preparation. It does not collect a case registry or track health data, and it does not claim clinical effectiveness. External products can change; recheck before publication or future product decisions.
