  - heading "Recovery can involve many parts of life" [level=2]
  - generic: Stroke effects and rehabilitation needs vary. Communication, movement, vision, thinking, mood, energy, confidence and daily activities may be part of a person’s experience. Not every effect is visible to other people, and no list can predict one person’s recovery.
  - heading "You and your carer may need different information" [level=2]
  - generic: People with stroke and the people supporting them may have different questions. Information needs can change over time, and it may help to revisit a topic with the care team instead of trying to absorb everything at once. The research behind this idea concerns stroke broadly, not dissection specifically.

  - heading "If someone comes with you" [level=2]
  - generic: You can decide whether another person joins and what you want them to do. They might listen, help you remember questions or take notes if you ask. The clinician should know how you prefer to communicate and who you want involved.
  - heading "Urgent changes" [level=2]
  - generic: If there are sudden symptoms that may be a stroke, contact local emergency services now. Do not use an app or wait for a routine appointment to assess them.
  - heading "Sources and limits" [level=2]
  - generic: The linked adult stroke rehabilitation guideline is written for its own jurisdiction and health system. It is a source for discussion, not a promise of local services or eligibility.
