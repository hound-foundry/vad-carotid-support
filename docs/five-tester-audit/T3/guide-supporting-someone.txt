  - generic: Ask what would help today instead of guessing. A person may want help with one task, company, quiet time or no help right now. Their preferences may change from day to day.

  - generic: •
  - generic: Ask whether the person wants you to attend and what role they want you to take.
  - generic: •
  - generic: Let the person answer first unless they ask you to speak for them.
  - generic: •
  - generic: Agree whether you should take notes or help remember questions.
  - generic: •
  - generic: Leave time for breaks and communication needs.
  - generic: •
  - generic: Ask before sharing their health information with anyone else.

  - heading "Your needs matter too" [level=2]
  - generic: Supporting someone can bring uncertainty, practical pressure and emotional strain. You can ask the care team or a local carers’ organisation what support is available to you. Evidence about which carer support programmes work best remains limited, so ask what fits your circumstances rather than assuming one service suits everyone.
