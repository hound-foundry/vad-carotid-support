What the words mean
A cervical artery dissection is a problem in the wall of an artery in the neck. The carotid arteries and vertebral arteries carry blood toward the brain. A dissection can affect either type of artery; the location and effects vary from person to person.
VAD means vertebral artery dissection. In this app, CAD means carotid artery dissection. The carotid and vertebral arteries in the neck carry blood toward the brain. A clinician can explain which artery is involved in an individual case.
