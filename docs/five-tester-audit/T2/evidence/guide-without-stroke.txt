Dissection without a diagnosed stroke
A pathway for people whose dissection was not accompanied by a diagnosed stroke. About 1 min read.

Your experience still matters
People’s symptoms, test findings, care and recovery differ. Not having a diagnosed stroke does not mean questions or ongoing effects are unimportant. This page is a prompt for discussion, not a prediction about what will happen.

Research limits
A published quality-of-life study can describe a particular group at a particular time. It cannot establish how any one person will feel or recover. The linked study is older and requires interpretation alongside newer evidence.
