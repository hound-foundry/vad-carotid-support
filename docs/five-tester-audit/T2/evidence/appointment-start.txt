Where the data lives
On the web, this app uses this browser profile’s local storage. It is not encrypted by this app and may be exposed to people or software with access to the profile or device. On iOS and Android, this app uses Expo SecureStore. Browser, device, operating-system and backup behavior varies; local storage alone does not guarantee privacy. Export sends a copy to the destination you choose. Deleting here may not remove an exported or backed-up copy.
