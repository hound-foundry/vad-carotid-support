## Assessment

The useful fit is the combination of labelled large-text controls, strong contrast, explicit previews before save/export, optional fields, and local-only draft continuity. My main friction is the inner menu scroll at narrow size and the urgent-help row partly cropped in the landscape focus-scrolled screenshot. A second interruption inconsistency is that Escape leaves the card delete confirmation open while sheet cancellation was recorded via Escape; the card still exposes a focused Cancel button. A first improvement would be to keep the urgent link and current keyboard focus fully visible at short landscape sizes, then align Escape behavior across confirmation dialogs.

Confirmed faults: none established as a critical or task-blocking fault in this evidence set. Usability barriers: menu content scroll/clipping at 320 px and partly cropped urgent row in the short-landscape capture; Escape behavior differs between card and sheet deletion. Preferences: compact branding, strong contrast, visible focus, large text, and generous labelled controls suit this perspective. Uncertainties: no direct T3 browser actuation; screenshots/DOM/records were produced by different current-round sessions; visible route presence does not independently prove every control's keyboard activation.
