# Dissection Companion design brief

Dissection Companion is a free, English first information and preparation website for adults with vertebral or carotid artery dissection and the people supporting them. The first useful action is choosing one of four calm tasks: understand dissection, prepare an appointment, make a card, or help someone.

The visual direction is calm, professional, human and quietly expressive. It keeps the established blue palette, rounded surfaces, plain language, four step card creator and separate practical/milestone cards. Card artwork can be more personal than the interface, while the interface stays restrained.

Accessibility is a design constraint: large readable type, strong focus, 48px targets, high contrast, dark and high contrast themes, reduced motion, reflow at narrow widths and short predictable steps. The design should reduce memory and attention demands and keep users in control of optional details, exports and deletion.

The implementation remains in the existing Expo web and native structure. Content is draft information pending clinical and lived experience review. No visual treatment implies a diagnosis, clinical endorsement, physical engraving or protection from migraine, fatigue or trauma reactions.

Current direction: layered but opaque surfaces, restrained shadows, colour coded task accents, a compact card creator progress rail, curated design metadata and full preview/export parity. Remove decorative effects whenever they compete with reading, focus or recovery from interruption.
