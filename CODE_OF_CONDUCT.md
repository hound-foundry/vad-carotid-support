# Community conduct

This project welcomes patients, carers, clinicians, researchers, translators and interested contributors. Treat people with care and respect, including when their experiences differ from your own.

Do not shame, threaten, harass, discriminate against or pressure people. Do not present personal anecdotes as proof or tell someone what diagnosis, medicine, procedure or treatment they need. Respect privacy: do not post anyone’s health information, even if you believe it is helpful.

Project maintainers may edit or remove content that exposes private information, requests individual medical advice or creates a foreseeable safety risk. For immediate danger, contact local emergency services; GitHub is not monitored for emergencies.

Report conduct concerns privately through repository contact options. Be factual and avoid unnecessary health details. The project is volunteer-led; response times are not guaranteed.
